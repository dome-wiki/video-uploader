Dome Video Uploader

A personal, internal tool. Not offered to the public.

Dome Video Uploader is a command-line tool that uploads videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to YouTube channels operated by the developer. It is used only by the developer, for the developer's own channels and the developer's own original content.

What it does

  • Reads a finished video file, its title and description, and an optional thumbnail from the developer's local computer.
  • Uploads them with the YouTube Data API v3 (videos.insert, thumbnails.set).
  • Checks the signed-in channel with channels.list (mine=true) and refuses to upload if it is not the intended channel.
  • Declares altered or synthetic content (containsSyntheticMedia) when a video uses synthetic narration.

What it does not do

  • It does not access any other user's account or channel.
  • It does not read, collect, store or share viewer data, comments or analytics.
  • It has no web interface and no other users.
